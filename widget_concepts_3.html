Floating chat bubble

A small tab in the corner of the report page, the pattern people already recognize from support-chat widgets. Stays out of the way until someone wants it; expands into a compact Q&A panel with suggested starter questions.

Best if
  • You want something unobtrusive
  • Report page also has other content
  • Fastest to embed (one script tag)
Watch for
  • Easy to miss on first visit
  • Small panel = shorter answers read best

Inline "Ask This Report" panel

A dedicated card embedded directly in the page flow, right below the report summary or download button. More visible than a bubble — it signals "this report is interactive" the moment someone lands on the page — and gives more room for longer, well-formatted answers.

Best if
  • This page's main job is the report
  • You want to visibly promote the tool
  • Answers may need more space (lists, quotes)
Watch for
  • Takes up permanent page real estate
  • Slightly more design work to match site styling

What do you recommend for funders?
Three headline recommendations: (1) provide patient, unrestricted core funding to grassroots organizations with lighter reporting burdens; (2) fund public goods and catalytic "first-loss" blended finance where private markets won't go; (3) invest in shared data infrastructure and research to rebuild the institutional memory lost with USAID. 📄 Recommendations for Funders, p.117

Sidebar companion (report + chat side by side)

A persistent side panel that pairs a mini table of contents with the Q&A tool — good for a standalone "read/explore the report" page rather than the summary landing page. Lets someone jump to a section or ask a question, and highlights where in the report an answer came from.

Best if
  • You build a dedicated report-reading page
  • You want browsing + Q&A in one place
  • Report has clear, citable sections (this one does — 10 findings)
Watch for
  • Most build effort of the three
  • Needs its own page layout, not just a widget on the existing page
